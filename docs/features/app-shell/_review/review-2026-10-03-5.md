### R4-1 edge cases (checked by the reviewer's probe)

- **Retry pressed twice:**
  - While a retry is in flight, the SCR-93 button is busy (`UnavailablePage.tsx:20,28`).
  - After a retry fails again, the failure hands out a new closure whose `revertedTo` is correct.
  - If another tab then picks a theme, the next retry sends nothing.
- **Another tab picks the theme the failure reverted to, or picks a different one and switches back:** the retry goes ahead. That is acceptable. The check compares state, not history: nothing newer is overwritten, and the Owner's Retry is the newest explicit act.
- **Blocked storage:** `currentChoice()` falls back to `blockedStorageChoice`, and with no storage events the check is always true. Correct.
- **Superseded sign-in or session-ended failure:** its retry is a no-op, which is harmless. `FailureBoundary.tsx:27-39` navigates away for those routes and never stores the retry.
